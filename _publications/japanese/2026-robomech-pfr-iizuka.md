This paper presents the conceptual and system-level design of a planar floating robot (PFR) equipped with compressed-air thrusters and a robotic manipulator for ground-based emulation of free-floating spacecraft dynamics.

The proposed platform integrates an air-bearing mechanism, an onboard pneumatic propulsion system, a robotic manipulator, and sensing capabilities to reproduce planar translational and rotational motion under near-frictionless conditions.

The design provides an experimental foundation for future validation of proximity operations, spacecraft capture, and detumbling strategies, including multi-robot cooperative experiments.
