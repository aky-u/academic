This paper presents the development of a dual-arm robotic manipulator and a planar air-floating experimental platform for investigating the coupled dynamics of free-floating space robots.

The developed system integrates planar three-degree-of-freedom manipulators, six-axis force/torque sensors, ROS 2-based control, and an air-bearing mechanism to emulate microgravity conditions in a two-dimensional environment.

Experimental results demonstrate that manipulator motion induces translational and rotational reactions of the floating base, providing a foundation for future validation of generalized Jacobian-based control, coordinated manipulation, and space debris capture strategies.
