This paper presents SpaceDyn ver. 3.0, an updated dynamics computation library for free-floating space robots with enhanced ROS/ROS 2 integration. The library is provided as a ROS 2 package and supports the computation of generalized Jacobian matrices, inertia matrices, and other dynamics quantities.

Its capabilities are demonstrated through Gazebo simulations, including end-effector trajectory tracking using the generalized Jacobian matrix and impedance-based contact control for reducing impact forces during target capture.
