This paper presents a model predictive control (MPC) framework for coordinated control of a spacecraft's thrusters and robotic manipulator during approach and manipulation of a tumbling, non-cooperative target. The proposed approach accounts for dynamic coupling between the spacecraft base and manipulator, allowing thruster inputs to compensate for reaction forces generated by arm motion.

Numerical simulations demonstrate stable relative-motion tracking, sequential approach, and precise end-effector positioning, achieving sub-millimeter tracking accuracy for both the spacecraft base and manipulator.
