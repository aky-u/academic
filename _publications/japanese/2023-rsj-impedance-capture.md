This paper proposes a space debris capture strategy for a dual-arm free-floating robot by combining impedance-controlled repeated contact with caging. The proposed method gradually reduces the rotational motion of a tumbling target before capture. Numerical simulations demonstrate a 66% reduction in peak contact force compared with direct caging.
